# Converted block on terraces 5 and 3 — what independent sources say (checked 2 Oct 2026)

Satellite result being checked: about 76 ha at the north tip of terrace 5 (centre 33.9445 N, 74.8457 E) and about
11 ha on terrace 3 went from vegetated to persistently bare; first large opening 2017–2018.
On Landsat the block had yearly p90 NDVI of 0.44–0.65 in every usable year 1993–2016, then 0.31 (2017), 0.24 (2018), 0.22 (2025).

## Place
Google Maps labels around the block: Kultreh, Tumchi Nowpora, Auwan Pora, Wadipora–Kuzwera Rd, and a point
"KOKA BRICK KILN [Brick No. K-11]". Administrative name in the documents below: Rangeen Kultreh, tehsil Chadoora, district Budgam.

## What matches and what does not
- **Matches:** the land use (brick kilns on karewa), the village, and the 2017 start for at least one kiln (source 1) all agree with the satellite onset of 2017–2018.
- **Does not match:** source 3 says the ~24 kilns date from 2003–2012 with nothing new in 2013–2022. The satellite record shows this
  block fully vegetated until 2016. Either the older kilns stand somewhere else in the village, or the news timeline is wrong.
  Source 1 (an official filing) sides with the satellite. Not resolved.
- Update, 7 October 2026: the tribunal filing (OA 364/2024) and the district list of kilns (status report in OA 594/2022, 4 October 2023) have been read in full; 2017, the closure order of 10-09-2018 and the 20 kilns within 1 km are as stated. The district list has six entries for Kultreh, three of them not yet built, so it does not support two dozen older kilns under this village's name. The press reports were checked against extracts.

## Saffron
The v1 "likely saffron" polygons (14 polygons, 225 ha, all around 33.96–34.02 N, 74.92–74.97 E) are 6.3 km or more from this block.
Only 0.2 ha of all converted pixels lie within 1 km of them. So the surviving loss is **not** on or next to the mapped saffron land.
(Source 5 lists Kultreh and Kuzwera among villages where saffron growing declined; that is about the past and is not something the 2025 saffron index can see.)
