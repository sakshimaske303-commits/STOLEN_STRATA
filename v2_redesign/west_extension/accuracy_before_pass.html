Third pass: what was at this point BEFORE (oldest clear image between 2009 and 2015)?

 

Open accuracy_sample_points.kml in Google Earth Pro. Double-click a point number, then use the clock icon (historical imagery) and slide back to the oldest clear image from 2009–2015. Answer what the centre of the point was THEN: vegetated (field, orchard, grass, trees), not_vegetated (kiln, bare ground, building, road), or unclear / no image. Type the year of the image you used.
