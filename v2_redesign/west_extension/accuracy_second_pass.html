Second pass: is the centre of the map inside a brick-kiln field?

 

The map opens a little zoomed out. One question only. I answer yes if the centre point lies inside the area a kiln uses: a kiln, rows of drying bricks, a clay pit, or bare worked ground with a kiln or brick rows close by on the same patch of bare land, no if the bare ground or road at the centre has nothing to do with a kiln (a fallow field, a village road, a building plot, a river bank). and unclear if I cannot tell.
